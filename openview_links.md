# OpenView Links to NIAID Blueprint-compatible metadata specifications

This page contains links to the OpenView display of NIAID Blueprint-compatible metadata specifications. To ensure you are seeing the most recent links, you may wish to visit the [CEDAR folder containing the specifications](https://cedar.metadatacenter.org/dashboard?folderId=https:%2F%2Frepo.metadatacenter.org%2Ffolders%2Fe9204d47-c9a0-4d61-83c5-ea27c7a36520). (A free CEDAR account is required, you can register when you click on that link.) You can click on any green box with an upward arrow to view that item in OpenView.
